Title: Linux/Xorg/OpenGL Digital Signage Player

The player is the software running on each signage screen: it fetches the scheduled content and renders images and video, continuously and unattended.

I developed the player on top of MultiQ's own Linux distribution, using Xorg and OpenGL for image display and VAAPI for hardware-accelerated video playback.

Implemented in C++, with mplayer for playback support and source in SVN.
