Title: Water and Electricity Cost Distribution

In a housing association everyone pays for cold water, hot water and electricity through the monthly fee, which means careful households subsidise wasteful ones. Individual metering lets the cost follow actual consumption, including in the shared washrooms.

I designed, developed and maintained the system that reads the meters and distributes the costs across apartments and washrooms. I also added encrypted communication for the integration with Bewator's access control system, which governs who may use the washrooms and when.

Implemented in C++ and PHP on a Linux/Apache/MySQL stack, with source in SVN deployed manually to the Linux server. Planning followed Kanban.
