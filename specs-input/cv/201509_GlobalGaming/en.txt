Title: Casino Site Web Application

The casino site is where players find games, register, deposit and play. It sits on top of the group's internal APIs for user management, payments and games, and has to work in several languages and rank in search.

I designed and built it as a fullstack developer, including multi-language support, SEO (title, description, canonical and alternate tags) and a GA/GTM data layer for analytics. I started on the code and grew into the Team Lead role for a team that expanded past ten people, eventually taking on management and part-time product ownership as well.

The frontend was a MarionetteJS/BackboneJS single-page application with Bootstrap, bundled with Webpack. The backend was an Express BFF with MongoDB and Mongoose. The system was deployed manually from SVN to company-owned infrastructure. Planning followed Scrum.
