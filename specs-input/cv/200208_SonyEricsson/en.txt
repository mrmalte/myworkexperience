Title: MMS

MMS is how Sony Ericsson's feature phones sent and received picture and multimedia messages, carried over WAP.

I was module architect for the MMS module, responsible for its design, implementation and maintenance. I stayed on the same module when Sony Ericsson took me on as an employee in January 2004.

Implemented in C on the phone platform, with source in ClearCase. Planning followed a waterfall process.
