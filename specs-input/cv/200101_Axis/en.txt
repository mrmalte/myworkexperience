Title: Canon BMLinkS Prototype

BMLinkS is a Japanese industry consortium standard for finding and using office equipment on a network, similar in purpose to UPnP. Canon needed a print application that spoke it.

I designed and developed the prototype print application.

Implemented in C with XML, SOAP, SSDP and GENA on embedded Linux, with source in CVS.

Development followed an iterative process inspired by Extreme Programming (XP), close to what is now called Kanban.
